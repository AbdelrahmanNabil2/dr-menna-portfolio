# How to update your website

You can change any text or add new photos yourself. You do not need to write code.

## Open the editor
1. Go to your website address and add `/admin` at the end.
   Example: `https://yourname.github.io/dr-menna-portfolio/admin/`
2. Click **Sign In Using Access Token**, paste your key, and sign in. (Your key was given to you when the site was set up. Keep it private.)
3. Click **Website**, then **All website content**.

## Change text
1. Click the section you want (for example **About me**). It opens.
2. Change the words.
3. Click **Save** at the top. The editor closes, and your website updates in 1 to 2 minutes.

## Add a new job, course or certificate
- **A new job or training:** open **Work experience**, find **Roles and experience**, click **Add roles and experience**, and fill in the boxes. Drag the item up or down to change the order.
- **A new course or certificate:** open **About me**, find **Education, courses and certificates**, click add, and write the year and the details.
- **A new skill:** open **Skills**, open the group, click add, and type it.

## Add a photo to "Selected work"
1. Open **Case photos**, then **Photo gallery**, then click add.
2. Upload the photo. Portrait photos like your current ones look best.
3. Write the **title** that shows under the photo (for example *Try-in*).
4. Write the **case name**. Use exactly the same name for every photo of the same case, for example *Complete denture*. The filter buttons on the website are made from these names, so a new case name creates a new button by itself.
5. Click **Save**. Drag photos up or down to change the order.

Your signature is already on each photo, so keep adding it before you upload. The website does not add it for you.

## Add a before and after case
1. Open **Case photos**, then **Cases**, then click add.
2. Write the case title, upload the **Before** and **After** photos, and add a short description.
3. Click **Save**. The website shows a slider that visitors can drag to compare.

**Before you add a patient photo:** you must have the patient's written permission and your supervisor's approval. Hide the patient's face and any name or identifying detail. Use the same angle for both photos so the slider lines up.

Photos from your phone are fine. They are shrunk automatically.

## Remove something
Open the item and use the delete (bin) button, or clear the text. For a phone number you do not want public, empty the **Phone** box under **Contact**.

## If something goes wrong
- **Did my change publish?** Wait 2 minutes and refresh the website.
- **Made a mistake?** Every change is saved in GitHub. Ask whoever set up the site to restore the previous version.
- **Cannot sign in?** Your key may have expired or been deleted. Ask for a new one.
